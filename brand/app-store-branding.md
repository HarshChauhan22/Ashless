# App Store Branding — Ashless

**Revision 2026-09-16**: rewritten for the rename from "Jeb" to **Ashless** (`docs/project/DECISIONS.md`). Same underlying brand voice and structure as before (see `brand-strategy.md` for the tone principles, which the rename doesn't change) — only the wordplay moved from "pocket" to "ash/residue," matching the new name and the new rising-stroke mark (`logo-concepts.md`).

## App icon

Rounded-square field, warm charcoal easing into gold (`#4B4640` → `#E7B23D`), the rising-stroke symbol reversed to cream at center. Reads as warm and resolved rather than clinical — sits apart from the sea of blue/white health-app icons and green meditation-app icons on any Play Store category page. See `assets/ashless-app-icon.svg`.

## Short description (Play Store, ≤80 chars)

Primary: **"Beat the craving. Keep the cash. Nothing left but what you saved."** (68 chars)
Alt A: **"Every cigarette leaves ash. This leaves savings instead."** (59 chars)
Alt B: **"The app that turns a smoking urge into money you keep."** (56 chars)

## Long description — direction (not final copy)

Structure, in order:
1. **Hook (1–2 lines):** open on the emotional line — "You felt the urge. Instead of a cigarette, you kept the money." Lead with the feeling, not the feature list.
2. **How it works (3 steps, plain language):** feel the urge → open the Digital Smoking Room and set your usual quantity like you're really buying → that exact amount goes into your own savings instead.
3. **What you get:** running savings total, streaks, milestone unlocks, an honest craving log (not a shame log) — money and momentum, not medical tracking.
4. **Trust/privacy line:** no data sold, no health-insurer sharing, private by default — important in India where health-app data trust is low and this product must never feel like it reports on you.
5. **Tone close:** one more restatement of the core line, e.g. "A cigarette leaves ash behind. This leaves you richer instead."

Avoid in long-copy: "cessation," "nicotine replacement," "relapse," "patient," "disease," "withdrawal symptoms" — all pull the listing toward pharma/health-portal territory the brief rejects. Prefer: "urge," "craving," "ritual," "streak," "win," "kept."

## Screenshot headline style

Every screenshot headline completes the same sentence pattern in first person, so the set reads as one story across the gallery:

1. "I wanted to smoke. I kept ₹200 instead." (craving screen)
2. "12 urges beaten. ₹2,840 kept." (savings dashboard)
3. "The 2 minutes that used to cost me. Now they pay me." (Digital Smoking Room flow)
4. "No lectures. Just the number going up." (milestone/streak screen)
5. "No ash. No smoke. Just what's left in your account." (closing screenshot)

Visual treatment: bold headline set in the display font over a real (blurred/simplified) product screenshot, gradient accent bar (charcoal→gold, matching the app icon), no stock photography of cigarettes or "sad smoker" imagery.

## Launch messaging

**Campaign line:** *"Har cigarette raakh chhod jaati hai. Yeh paisa chhodta hai."* ("Every cigarette leaves ash behind. This leaves money.") — Hinglish for India-first channels, with a clean English equivalent for pan-India/English-first placements: *"Cigarettes leave ash. This leaves savings."*

**Launch narrative beat:** don't lead with "quit smoking app #4,382" — lead with the mechanic no one else has (the ritual-replacement + real money redirect). Press/influencer angle: "The app that doesn't ask you to fight the craving — it asks you to get paid for it."

**Where this plays first:** India Android-first (per the brief), so launch messaging should assume Hindi/Hinglish comprehension by default with clean English fallback — not the reverse.
