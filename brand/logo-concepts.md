# Logo Concepts — Ashless

**Revision 2026-09-16 (rename)**: this document previously described a "Kept Pocket" symbol built for the name **Jeb**. The app was renamed to **Ashless** in a later session (see `docs/project/DECISIONS.md`), and the mark changed with it — a pocket shape doesn't carry any meaning for a name about ash and residue. The original Jeb naming research is preserved, marked superseded, in `name-research.md` and `brand-strategy.md` — not rewritten here, since editing history to pretend it always said "Ashless" would misrepresent how the decision was actually made.

**Revision 2026-09-16 (symbol redesign)**: the checkmark-based "Rising Stroke" symbol described below was replaced same-day with **"The Broken Loop"** after review — the checkmark read as a generic UI icon rather than something specific to this product. Several checkmark refinements and literal cigarette/ember treatments were also explored and rejected before landing on the loop. The wordmark, combination-mark layout, and app-icon field treatment are unchanged; only the symbol shape changed. The old checkmark description is kept below, marked superseded, for the same reason the Jeb history is kept.

The mark needs to say, without a single literal cigarette, lung, ash particle, or medical cross: resolved, done, clean, kept. The current organizing idea is **the broken loop** — habits run on a loop (cue, routine, reward); the mark is that loop, drawn almost whole and deliberately left open, colored so the break itself is where the story resolves.

Vector files for all four marks below are in `assets/` as SVG (hand-built directional concepts — meant for a designer to refine in Figma/Illustrator before production use, not production-final artwork).

---

## The core symbol: "The Broken Loop" (current)

A ring built from eight short arcs sharing one center and radius, sweeping 320° clockwise and leaving a 40° gap near the top. Color runs the same brand progression as before — muted ash-grey (`#8A8074`) at the start of the sweep, through transitional amber-bronze, resolving into brand gold (`#E7B23D`) at the arc's end, immediately adjacent to the gap. Ends are cut flat (`stroke-linecap="butt"`), not rounded, so the break reads as a clean snap rather than a soft fade.

Why this works against the brief:
- **Not a checkmark, not literal ash/smoke/cigarette** — a distinct silhouette from anything else in the category; nothing to mistake for a generic "done" UI icon.
- **The habit loop, specifically** — cue/routine/reward is the standard behavioral-science framing for a habit; drawing that loop and then breaking it open is a shape only this kind of product would plausibly own.
- **The break is the resolution, not an afterthought** — the gold end sits right at the gap, so the moment the loop stops closing on itself is also the moment the color finishes resolving. Grey is the loop still running; gold is the loop interrupted.
- **Whole-but-open reads as "recovering," not "broken"** — 320° of a circle is still unmistakably a ring at a glance; the gap doesn't read as damage, it reads as a deliberate opening.

File: `assets/ashless-symbol.svg`

## Superseded: "The Rising Stroke" (checkmark, replaced 2026-09-16)

A single thick, rounded-cap stroke in a checkmark shape (short leg down-right, long leg up-right), rendered with a linear gradient that runs warm ash-grey (`#8A8074`) at the short leg through a transitional amber-bronze (`#B08A4E`) and resolves into brand gold (`#E7B23D`) at the tip of the long leg. Replaced because, in review, it read as a generic checkmark/"done" UI icon rather than something specific to a quit-smoking product — the same critique that had been raised, and accepted, against a literal crossed-out-cigarette icon.

## Wordmark

Lowercase "ashless," set in a bold, rounded geometric sans (direction: Cabinet Grotesk / Poppins Rounded / General Sans — same type direction the brand already committed to, unchanged by the rename). Lowercase throughout, same rationale as before: approachable, the way you'd text it to a friend, not a clinical acronym.

A small filled gold dot follows the wordmark — carried over unchanged from the prior "jeb." wordmark's device — a satisfied full stop that doubles as the same coin/value color used in the symbol's gradient tip, tying the two together.

## App icon

The same broken-loop symbol, reversed to flat cream (`#FFF9F0`, no gradient on the mark itself — the field carries the color story instead), centered on a rounded-square field carrying a diagonal version of the same story: warm charcoal (`#4B4640`, an "ash" tone deliberately darker/duller than any in-product UI color, so the icon reads as distinct from the app's own screens) at top-left, easing through the same transitional bronze into gold (`#E7B23D`) at bottom-right. Symbol scaled to ~82% of the full-size mark to sit inside Android's adaptive-icon ~66% safe zone so it survives circular/squircle masking without clipping the ring's open ends.

## What we deliberately avoided

- No literal ash flecks, embers, smoke wisps, or cigarette silhouettes anywhere in the identity system — same restraint the brand already committed to under the previous name, unchanged by the rename.
- No lungs, no medical cross, no crossed-out cigarette icon — those read as clinical/punitive, not as the "kept, not lectured" tone the brand voice wants.
- No closed cages, locks, or boxes with lids — the brief calls for freedom, not restriction. The broken-loop symbol is a near-circle, but the point of the mark is the gap: it's a loop that's been opened, never a shape that fully encloses anything.
- No literal "A" lettermark or monogram — a single-letter mark would have to fight the wordmark's own lowercase "a," and would read as generic tech-brand shorthand rather than something specific to this product's story.
