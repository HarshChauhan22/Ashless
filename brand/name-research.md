# Name Research — India-First Smoking Cessation App

**Superseded 2026-09-16 — kept for history, not the current decision.** This document's research led to "Jeb" being chosen (see `brand-strategy.md`'s final recommendation). The app was later renamed to **Ashless** in a separate, subsequent naming round done directly with the project owner — a different brief (English, smoke-themed, deliberately not Hindi-rooted) than the one this document was researched against. See `docs/project/DECISIONS.md` for the actual current decision. Nothing below has been edited to fit the new name; it's an accurate record of the first round, not a live brand asset.

**Core concept:** A smoker feels a craving → opens a Digital Smoking Room → recreates the purchase ritual (selects quantity as if buying cigarettes) → the equivalent cash moves into a Quit Wallet instead.
**Core emotional line:** *"I wanted to smoke, but instead I kept the money."*

**Note on verification:** Nothing below has been checked against the Trademark Registry, USPTO/EUIPO, Google Play, or domain registrars. "Trademark risk" scores are conceptual judgment calls (does the word collide with a known existing brand I'm aware of, or is it generic-word-crowded) — not a legal search. Before locking a name, run an actual TM class search (Class 9/42 software, Class 44 health services) and check Play Store / App Store / domain / Instagram / X handle availability.

### 3. Freedom-focused
Unbound · Untethr · Liftoff · ExhaleFree

### 4. Habit-breaking
Rewire · Loopbreak · PatternBreak · Habitloop

### 5. Craving-focused
Craveless · UrgeOut · HoldOn · Waitout

### 6. Behavioral transformation
Pivot · Instead · Redirect · Swapit

### 7. Indian / Hinglish-rooted, globally usable
Jeb (जेब — "pocket," where the saved money literally lives) · Dum (दम — a puff/drag, but also breath, stamina, courage — "dum hona" = to have guts) · Rok (रोक — "stop") · RukJa (रुक जा — "stop/hold on," imperative & casual) · Saans (सांस — "breath") · Thamo (थांबो — "stop/wait," Marathi) · DumDaar (दमदार — "gutsy, has courage")

**Total: 34 candidates.**

Immediately ruled out at the brainstorm stage: anything with "smoke," "nicotine," "lung," or "health" as the dominant root — these pull the brand toward the pharma/government-portal register the brief explicitly rejects. Also ruled out: cutesy diminutives (Quitster, Puffly) that read as children's-app territory.

**Flags worth knowing before you fall in love with a name:**
- **Dum** — real risk: to an English-only reader/speaker, "Dum" reads and often gets typed/searched as **"dumb."** That's a serious ASO and word-of-mouth liability for a product meant to scale globally, even though the Hindi wordplay is genuinely the cleverest idea on this list. Great for an India-only campaign name or sub-brand ("Dum se Dum tak" as a feature name), risky as the master brand.
- **Unbound** — there is an existing sexual-wellness brand called Unbound and a UK publishing platform called Unbound. Conceptual crowding, not confirmed legal conflict.
- **Instead** — "Instead" was a real feminine-hygiene product brand (Instead Softcup). Also just a very common English word — expect Play Store search crowding and a weak trademark position without a strong wordmark/logo to compensate.
- **Rewire** — already used by an existing fintech app for migrant workers, plus multiple habit/neuroscience apps. Crowded space.

---

## Shortlist Verdict

The strongest three, in order, are **Jeb → Rok → Instead**. Full reasoning is in `brand-strategy.md` and the final call in the main brand doc — but in short: Jeb is the only name on this list where the *word itself* is the money mechanic (pocket = where the cash stays), it's short and pronounceable everywhere, it has no medical/childish/cigarette-brand register, and it doesn't carry a known direct conflict the way Unbound, Rewire, and Instead do.
